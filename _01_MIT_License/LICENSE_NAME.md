# MIT License

## 📜 Overview

The **MIT License** is one of the most popular and permissive open-source software licenses. It was originally developed at the **Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)** and is known for its simplicity and minimal restrictions.

## 🔑 Key Characteristics

- **Permissive**: Very few restrictions on reuse
- **Simple**: Short and easy to understand
- **Compatible**: Compatible with most other licenses including GPL
- **Business-Friendly**: Companies love it because it doesn't require sharing modifications
- **No Copyleft**: Derivative works can be distributed under different terms

## 🧠 Developer's Perspective

> "If you want maximum freedom for both yourself and anyone who uses your code, and you don't care if someone takes your code and makes it proprietary — the MIT License is your best friend."

The MIT License is ideal when you want your code to be used as widely as possible without any legal friction. It's the **"I don't care what you do with it, just give me credit"** license.
